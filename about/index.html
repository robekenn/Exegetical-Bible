Why I built Exegetical Bible

I love studying the Bible and using tools like an interlinear Bible, commentaries, and different translations. I used to jump between sites to find them, so I built Exegetical Bible to bring those resources together in one place.

I plan to keep improving the site based on what helps you study. I want your Bible to stay at the center of your reading, with Exegetical as a tool to help you better understand the passage in front of you.

A note about AI

AI played a substantial role in developing the website. The Bible verses, commentary, and transliterations you read here come from the sources credited on the site. None of that material is AI-written. I don’t believe AI should interpret the Bible, and I won’t add AI features to Exegetical Bible.
