Changelog

New features and fixes, grouped by release day.

November 6, 2026

  • Changing your password signs out all devices on your account.
  • Password reset emails now open a dedicated page to choose a new password.
  • Improved password entry with a visibility toggle and clearer length checks when setting a password.
  • Added an About the creator page with a link in the reader footer.
  • Made the Return to the Bible button readable on the 404 page.
  • Restored the Save note label in notebook highlight editors.
  • Opening a related verse on phones now scrolls to that verse as Study closes.
  • Open saved notes directly on phones and download large notebooks in importable parts.
  • Improved account deletion confirmations, offline sign-out, moderation evidence, and Bible provider safeguards.
  • Open translator footnotes and alternate readings from the † marker beside annotated verses.
  • Rebalanced the five Study tabs so their labels have room on desktop and phone.
  • Preview cross-references in Study → Related and return to your starting passage after opening one.
  • Direct verse links keep the requested verse in view after the reader applies your saved appearance settings.

November 5, 2026

  • Passage links and notebook jumps scroll to the requested verse. Search and sharing now support verse ranges and single-chapter shorthand.
  • Highlight note drafts survive closing the notebook, note search accepts common range dashes, and book matches offer a verse-text search option.
  • Shared links keep their passage when you sign in. Translation changes preserve selections and explain missing coverage.
  • Compare shows each edition’s reference and lets you adjust it when verse alignment is uncertain.
  • Improved Study keyboard controls and reader contrast, corrected Testament grouping, and distinguished translations with matching titles.
  • Tap a selected verse again to remove it instantly. Notes and highlights can now apply to the entire selected passage.
  • Tap adjacent verses to select a passage, then double-tap a verse, selection, or highlight to open Study.
  • Added this changelog so you can see what changed each day. It uses the reader’s logo and follows your light or dark theme.
  • The loading screen and informational pages now follow your light or dark theme, including your signed-in preference.
  • The desktop reading button slides both side panels closed. Click it again to restore the panels you had open, or use the edge arrows to reopen them individually.
  • Books and Study can now slide open or closed using arrow handles on the edges of the desktop reader.
  • Fixed desktop Study panel clipping, kept the highlighter in the reading column, and aligned the search arrow with the search bar.
  • Removed the extra study hint below the mobile reader.
  • Polished notebook highlight buttons, fixed extra verse spacing, and moved both translation notices below the Compare cards.
